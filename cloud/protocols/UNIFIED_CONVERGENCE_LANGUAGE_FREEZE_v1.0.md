P1. Useful coarse-grainings will be those that are congruent with at least part of the underlying transformation grammar, not merely those that compress well.

P2. Structures that survive quotienting and still support held-out prediction will transfer more reliably than raw symbol/parameter coincidences.

## 9. Falsification conditions

- bridge success disappears under target-only or wrong-grammar controls;
- mappings require post-hoc semantic relabeling;
- different tracks require mutually incompatible definitions of the frozen primitives;
- the shell can fit every outcome equally well;
- quotient-survival does not improve held-out prediction;
- apparent invariants vanish under proper total accounting;
- or prospective cross-domain transfer repeatedly fails after adequate power/identifiability is demonstrated.

Repeated FAIL results are evidence and must remain in the master registry.

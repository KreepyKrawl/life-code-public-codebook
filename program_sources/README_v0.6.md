This continuation adds a spatial scientific contract between phenotype inference and visualization.

Core locks:
- THE PACKET, NOT THE PICTURE, IS THE PREDICTION.
- THE PIXEL MUST HAVE A PROVENANCE PATH.
- Unsupported visual detail is ghosted, artistic-only, or omitted.
- Spatial truth is scored against the frozen region packet.
- EXP-0002 remains sealed/quarantined.
